1. On May 27, 2026, a tech startup issues a bond with a face value of $1,000, an annual coupon rate of 5%, and a maturity of 4 years. If the market interest rate is 3% compounded annually, what is the closest price (to the nearest $1) that an investor should pay for the bond?
Explanation:
The bond pays $50 annually for 4 years and returns $1,000 at maturity.
Present value = PV(coupons) + PV(face value)
PV(coupons) = $50 × [1 − (1 + 0.03)−4]/0.03 ≈ $50 × 3.717 ≈ $186
PV(face value) = $1,000 × (1 + 0.03)−4 ≈ $1,000 × 0.886 ≈ $886
Total = $186 + $886 = $1,072
The closest answer is $1,077 (C).
